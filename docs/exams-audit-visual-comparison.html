4. Platform Archetypes — 5 distinct models

The 9 benchmarks cluster into 5 archetypes. Your site should pick one and execute it well.

A · Catalog + Calendar

Testbook, PendulumEdu — App-like, exams as first-class entities, sub-routes, calendar centerpiece.

  • Exam listing w/ counts + calendar
  • Per-exam detail w/ sub-routes
  • Sticky sidebar mega-nav (TB)
  • Test series + quizzes embedded
  • ← BEST FIT FOR YOU
B · News & Updates Hub

Adda247, SarkariResult — Content/SEO-first. Articles as exam pages. Notification-driven.

  • Latest articles feed as homepage
  • Mega-menu category nav
  • Article-based detail (long page)
  • State/vernacular depth
  • SarkariResult = pure notice aggregator
C · Course-Catalog

Physics Wallah, BYJU's — Polished UI, news-led, courses/batches are first-class, exams aren't.

  • News carousel + category sections
  • Per-category subroutes
  • Course/batch CTAs dominate
  • Counsellor / lead-capture widget
  • BYJU's adds OTP lead forms
D · Mock-Test Hub

Oliveboard, Smartkeeda — Test-series-first. Exam pages are gateways to mock tests.

  • Topper social proof (OB)
  • Score calculator (OB)
  • Category tabs → exam grid (OB)
  • Smartkeeda = pure question bank, no exam info
  • Performance analytics
E · Article Encyclopedia

StudyIQ — Wikipedia-style long-form. Strong SEO, weak UX. Niche (UPSC).

  • Course tiles as homepage
  • Article = exam page
  • Syllabus/PYQ PDF hubs in footer
  • No calendar, no tabs
  • Single-segment deep content

Recommendation: Adopt Archetype A (Testbook + PendulumEdu) — both use sub-routes like you should. Borrow: Adda247's news-feed density for Updates, Oliveboard's topper social proof + score calculator, PW's counsellor CTA, PendulumEdu's one-to-one mentoring angle.
