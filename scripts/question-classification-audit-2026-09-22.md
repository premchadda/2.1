## Method

  explanation and Hindi explanation (HTML stripped).
- Matches **phrase / format signatures** with AND-of-OR group semantics — never lone
  ambiguous tokens. (Reason the earlier regex pass produced junk such as English
  "Direct Speech" questions flagged as Quant because they contained the word "interest".)
- Reasoning _format_ signatures (e.g. "odd one out", "in a certain code") outrank
  topical keywords, so GK-flavoured reasoning questions stay in Reasoning.
- Every finding carries the matched evidence span + confidence for human review.
